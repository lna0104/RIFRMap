Comprehensive Antibiotic Resistance Database (CARD)

The Comprehensive Antibiotic Resistance Database (CARD) is a hand-curated resource developed to cover the full spectrum of antimicrobial resistance genes (ARGs). To be included, an AMR determinant must be reported in a peer-reviewed publication, have a corresponding sequence deposited in GenBank, and show clear experimental evidence of increased minimum inhibitory concentration (MIC) compared with control strains [1]. For Mycobacterium tuberculosis, additional curated mutation data were integrated from several major resistance catalogs, including CRyPTIC, ReSeqTB, and the WHO 2023 mutation catalog.

Comprehensive Resistance Prediction for Tuberculosis: an International Consortium (CRyPTIC)

The CRyPTIC Consortium generated a large-scale dataset by measuring quantitative MICs for 13 anti-tuberculosis drugs—including rifampicin— in over 15,000 globally collected M. tuberculosis isolates. Using multivariable linear mixed models alongside whole-genome sequencing, the study identified variants associated with increased MICs (resistance) and decreased MICs (hypersusceptibility) [2]. RIFRMap enables interactive exploration of this dataset with filters for effect size, variant count, adjusted p-value, and homoplasy.

Drug Resistance Associated Genes Database (DRAGdb)

DRAGdb is a manually curated repository of mutational data for drug resistance-associated genes across ESKAPE pathogens and other bacteria, with a particular focus on Mycobacterium tuberculosis. The database integrates PROVEAN scores to predict the functional impact of gene mutations [3].

Yang-RIF (2023)

The Yang-RIF dataset is based on a study by Yang and colleagues, who performed saturation mutagenesis of the rifampicin-binding site in E. coli RNA polymerase. They constructed single-residue rpoB mutants spanning positions 510–537 and 563–572 to systematically assess resistance phenotypes [4]. Within RIFRMap, users can explore this dataset by filtering mutations based on enrichment after rifampicin exposure.

Curated Data

This curated dataset was developed through a literature review of rifampicin resistance mutations in rpoB. Relevant studies were identified using targeted Google Scholar searches. Single amino acid substitutions reported to confer rifampicin resistance were manually extracted [5].
